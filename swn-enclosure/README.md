# Caveat

"sŵn" does not need an enclosure to work. A RPi + USB drive and a suitable PSU and you are off to the races.

But with an enclosure it is miles ahead, mainly because it looks better. The enclosure designed for the transport takes in consideration (1) mechanical isolation for the transport and (2) mechanical isolation for the entire enclosure, (3) proper wire routing, (4) provision of an eject button as well as (5) vibration reduction.

# Enclosure

The enclosure is designed to be fully 3D printed, and you only need a spool of PLA to get the most of it. Apart from the PLA, and obviously, a 3D printer, you need:

- CA glue;
- 8 m3 threaded inserts;
- 8 countersunk machine head m3x10 screws;
- 15 NBR 8x1.5 o-rings;
- 15 NBR 11x2 o-tings; and,
- some patience.

The models are distributed within indivudual folders to facilitate staggered printing.

## Feet

The enclosure uses three (3) feet, each consisting of 5 individual parts localted in the ```enclosure-foot``` folder.

Every part screws onto the other, with the exception of "Foot Screw" that needs to be glued to "Holder Top" by locating it in the top indent.

Once all dried up, "Holder Top" and "Holder Bottom" need to be partially screwed, and then have the "11x2" o-rings located alongside each protrusion. Once done, each o-ring needs to be stretched on the corresponding protrusion on "Foot Bottom" and then have "Foot Cover" screwed on.

Rinse and repeat two more times.

## Bottom

The bottom consists of three parts, located in the ```enclosure-case``` folder.

Glue "bottomLeft", "bottomMiddle" and "bottomRight" together and let it cure overnight.

## Top

The top consists of three parts, located in the ```enclosure-case``` folder.

Glue "topLeft", "topMiddle" and "topRight" together and let it cure overnight.

Once cured, use your method of choice to press in the m3 threaded inserts in the corresponding openings on the now completed enclosure top. There is some tolerance there, so no need to pursue apsolute flushness of the inserts.

## Transport

Similar to the enclosure feet, print out three sets of the files in the ```transport-isolator``` folder.

Once done, use the 8x1.5 o-rings and locate the assemblies on the protrusions on the bottom part of the enclosure.

Next, print out three copies of "Transport Clamp" from the ```enclosure-support``` folder, fit to the SuperDrive, and, once located on the tops of the transport isolators, glue in place.

# Settings

Fairly typical PLA settings, suit to your liking. What works for me is the following:

- 0.2mm layer height;
- minimal supports;
- gyroid infill at 15%;
- 2 wall loops.
